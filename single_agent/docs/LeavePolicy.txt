LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive Paid Time Off (PTO) based on years of service.

Vacation Leave
Employees may use vacation leave with manager approval.

Manager Approval

• Up to 3 consecutive days:
Manager approval required.

• More than 3 consecutive days:
Manager approval and HR approval required.

Sick Leave

Employees may use available sick leave for illness.

Medical documentation is required after three consecutive sick days.

Carry Forward

Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused PTO days each calendar year.

FMLA

Employees completing at least 12 months of service are eligible for FMLA leave.

FMLA provides up to 12 weeks of unpaid job-protected leave.

Bereavement Leave

Employees receive up to 5 paid bereavement days.

Military Leave

Military leave follows federal regulations.

Jury Duty

Employees receive paid leave while serving jury duty.
